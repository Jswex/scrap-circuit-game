# Scrap Circuit Prototype Brief

## Concept

A short top-down racing game where players collect strange, high-impact upgrades during a three-lap race against bots. The prototype ends when the player crosses the finish line.

## Learning Goal

Determine whether players find upgrade-driven interference more memorable and fun than ordinary speed boosts.

## Riskiest Assumption

Players will understand and intentionally use distinct upgrades while steering at race speed.

## First Playtest

Ask five players to complete two races without coaching. Observe whether they collect and activate upgrades, which upgrade produces the strongest reaction, and whether they voluntarily restart.

After each test, ask:

1. Which moment do you remember most?
2. What did each upgrade do?
3. Would you rather race more players, unlock more upgrades, or explore another track?

## Success Signal

At least four of five players use an upgrade intentionally, three can explain two upgrade effects, and two choose to race again without being prompted.
